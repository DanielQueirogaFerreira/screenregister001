Storage economics of a screen recorder

A stored frame costs about 103 kilobytes at WebP quality 0.7, and the recorder keeps roughly one frame per second while the screen is changing. That works out at 370 megabytes for every hour of active use, which no free tier will absorb over seven days.

The frames are already close to the efficiency frontier, so the saving has to come from how many are stored rather than how large each one is. Text is what degrades first when quality falls, and text is what both the reader and the OCR need.
