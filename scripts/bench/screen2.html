Storage economics
A stored frame costs about 103 KB at WebP quality 0.7, and the recorder keeps roughly one frame per second while the screen changes. That is 370 MB for every hour of active use. The frames sit close to the efficiency frontier, so the saving comes from how many are stored rather than how large each one is. Text degrades first when quality falls.
